% ---------------------------------------------------------------- Task 4: evaluation (measured numbers so far)
\textbf{Evaluation.} Every hold gesture was recognised after 0.52\,s on average (hold time 0.5\,s plus at most
one frame; 24 gestures in three runs); with the 70\,ms camera-to-screen latency, the screen reacts about
0.6\,s after the player raises the hands. The hold time and ``lower the hand first'' prevent repeated or
accidental triggers. In the replays, 6 of the 7 bonus stars were grabbed (86\,\%), on average 2.1\,s after they appeared; when both
players reach for the star at once, the closest hand gets it, so there is never a double award.
